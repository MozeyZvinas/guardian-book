Brand · the chosen mark · 01 Sentinel · 25 Sep 2026

Sentinel, developed.

The founder’s pick from the ten options, kept as drawn: ink head, pink crest, and the pink and blue tiles. Two changes only. The crest geometry is redrawn so every feather sits inside the mark’s box at every size — nothing is clipped any more. The main colours are white, black, pink and blue. Two side colours come from the bookstore screenshots — the deep green and a touch of orange — for the guardian header, chips and the streak; the night theme is black. The same screenshot lends its layout (black sheet, white sheet, one emphasised word, big numerals) and its typeface, Outfit; Direction K below shows it on all nine screens.
