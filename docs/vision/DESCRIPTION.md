# DESCRIPTION.md — how to describe Guardian Book (canonical; copy from here)

**v2 · 27 October 2026 · founder-directed, CTO-written.** Every pitch, form, post,
README, submission and prompt describes Guardian Book from this file. If a description
anywhere else disagrees, this file wins.

v2 adds what v1 undersold: the learner's dreams and interests, support, and the people
around them (docs/WHOLE_LEARNER.md). v2.1 (27 Oct) adds the living principle: it grows

**The rule:** never describe Guardian Book as a marking app, a homework helper, a tutor, a
chatbot or an "AI friend". Lead with the whole: one intelligence around every learner and
the people who support them. Marking is one feature among many and never the headline.

## Full (≈300 words)

Guardian Book is a lifelong learning intelligence that starts as your teacher, grows
into your mentor and never stops being your guardian. It begins in high school, Grades 8
to 12, where the choices that shape a career are made. It is built to grow with a person
through university, work, business and one day their own child.

At its centre is the Brain: a personal model of one learner, built from their real work
and their own goals. It knows how they learn, where they're stuck, what they love and
where they want to go. It turns that into what each person needs.

The Brain never stands still. Every interaction teaches it something. It updates with
every answer, re-forms the plan every
week, and changes as the learner changes from Grade 8 to university and work. It also
keeps up with the world: new opportunities, careers and skills. So the learner stays a
step ahead of the generic pace: next term's topics before the class reaches them, more
challenge the moment a topic is mastered, and deadlines seen early.

**For learners:**
- practice on real exam papers anywhere, even offline;
- feedback on every answer, on their own handwriting;
- a tutor that teaches the step instead of giving the answer;
- explanations to read, see or hear;
- a daily plan with room for life;
- their dreams turned into this month's step;
- the doors ahead: subject choices, programmes and admission scores, bursaries,
  careers with real demand, the business path, and the Olympiads, expos and
  programmes that fit their interests;
- one tap to reach a trusted adult or a helpline when they need someone;
- a verified record of everything they have achieved.

**For parents:** effort, progress and released schoolwork in one place; the gap in plain
language; exactly how to help this week; free help first; and a Brief that makes any
tutor or mentor useful from the first hour.

**For teachers:** scan a class set, confirm the AI's marks in minutes, see who needs help
with what (including who needs a person), and send each parent a personal note.

**For schools:** one platform for learning, results, support and parent communication,
with early warnings across every grade.

**For mentors, tutors and coaches:** briefed, vetted and booked, so every session starts
where the learner is.

It exists for the education-to-work gap: nearly half of young South Africans in the
labour force can't find work. Guardian Book makes sure no talent walks blind into a
closed door again, and no learner walks alone.

## The eight pillars (when you need the whole map)

1. **Learn anywhere:** official past papers shown exactly as printed; Pocket Mode for
   taxis and buses; offline sets; typed, photographed or handwritten-on-screen answers;
   self-marking.
2. **Feedback and teaching:** every answer explained step by step against the official
   memo; a tutor that teaches rather than tells; lessons to read, see or hear.
3. **The Brain, always growing:**
   - mastery evidence, today's plan, forecasts of where the learner is heading, and the
     reasons behind every suggestion;
   - it adapts with every answer, every week, every term and as the world changes;
   - it keeps the learner a step ahead: "Next up in class" previews, "Ready for more"
     stretch work and early deadlines;
   - the Growth timeline shows what changed and why.
4. **Dreams and opportunities:** the Me page (dreams, hobbies, causes); each dream made
   into a plan; the Opportunity Radar (Olympiads, expos, competitions, programmes,
   volunteering); a week with room to breathe.
5. **The doors:** subject choice with honest pros and cons, pathways and admission
   scores, bursaries and NSFAS deadlines, careers with real demand, the business path.
6. **Never alone:** "Help now" in one tap (helplines, a trusted adult, the school's
   support person); My people, the trusted adults the learner chooses; mentors, tutors
   and coaches who arrive already briefed; human-led support programmes in school. The
   AI connects learners to people; it is never the friend or the therapist.
7. **The circle around the learner:**
   - parents: effort, released schoolwork, the Help Plan, free and paid help, the Brief;
   - teachers: scan, mark, confirm, release, who needs help, personal notes;
   - schools: whole-school communication and support, early warnings.
8. **The record, for life:** a living CV of verified achievement inside and outside the
   classroom, owned by the learner, shared only by their choice (never with employers
   before 18). The same intelligence serves every stage, from first letters to a PhD or
   a business, and back to the start when the learner becomes a parent.

## What Guardian Book is not

Not a marking app. Not a chatbot. Not an AI friend or therapist. Not an answer vending
machine. Not an open tutoring marketplace. Not surveillance of children. Not a feed.

## Where marking fits

Marking is one capability inside pillars 2 and 7. It gives learners instant feedback,
gives teachers their time back, and produces the evidence the Brain learns from. In a
demo it can be a scene. In a description it is never the opening line.

## Where "grows with you" fits

It is the principle behind every pillar, not a feature: Guardian Book is never finished
and never fixed. **Every interaction teaches it:** each answer, question, goal and
deadline helps it understand the learner better and improve their chances of success,
and the learner can always see exactly what it learned (FEATURES §4.33, TECH §3a). Never
say "we track everything". "How they learn" means evidence of what works for this learner (pace,
errors, what helps, preferred format). It never means a "learning style" label. "A step
ahead" means ahead of the generic pace, alongside the teacher, never against them, and
never more hours (FEATURES §4.32).

## Where support fits

Support is pillar 6, and it is always people. The intelligence notices, prepares and
connects; trusted adults, teachers, mentors, trained counsellors and helplines do the
caring. The line: **"It never pretends to be your friend. It makes sure you're never
without one."**
