# DESIGN.md — Guardian Book Design Language (binding for all UI work)
**Owner: CTO session · v1: 11 July 2026 · Consumed by: W3+ (Claude Code reads this before ANY screen work, alongside PRD §7)**
Sources: founder's design brief (UX Peak A/B transcript, docs case), Mobbin research (use the MCP), 2026 trend synthesis (Tubik, UX Collective, index.dev — links in git commit).

## 0. Non-negotiables (override every trend)
**Sentinel identity (v2 — see §v2; founder decision 25 Sep 2026; the v1 chalkboard-green + gold identity is retired, its green lives on as the Guardian portal's side colour)** · WCAG AA contrast minimum · child-appropriate everything · performance floor = entry-level Android on mobile data (Core Web Vitals: LCP <2.5s on 3G-class, JS bundle budget per route, images lazy + compressed) · `prefers-reduced-motion` respected everywhere · no dark patterns, ever — we are a trust product for minors. When a trend fights these, the trend loses.

## 1. Trend rulings — the founder's top-5 list (2025 video brief) + 2026 synthesis

1. **Emotionally intelligent design (mascot) — ADOPT. This is the headline decision.** Guardian Book gets a companion character: **a guardian figure who stands watch over your practice** — celebrating marks, encouraging after wrong answers, gently worried when the streak slips. Rules: (a) designed for Gr 8–12 — memeable and cool like Duo, never babyish; (b) **supportive, never menacing** — our guardian worries, it does NOT get angry or guilt-trip (wellbeing rule; the Duo "angry" stage is explicitly off-brand for us); (c) v1 = simple SVG character with 5 states (celebrating / encouraging / waiting / concerned / proud), CSS/sprite animation — no heavy rigs; (d) appears at emotional moments (results, streaks, empty states, guardian-link screen), never blocking content. Species/name = founder's call (see board note); build v1 states species-agnostically silhouette-first if undecided.
   **1.1a — Secretary-bird guardrails (binding — F9 landed on Sentinel, 25 Sep 2026; Sentinel passes (i), (ii) and the colour half of (iii); A5 in §v2 covers the silhouette half):** (i) **Never heraldic.** The bird's meaning comes from the national coat of arms — its rendering must never imitate it: no uplifted-wings arms pose, no rising sun, shield, protea or heraldic framing, no state-gold treatment. Next to our "DBE · Nov 2024" source badges a coat-of-arms echo reads as a government product — a trust and legal problem (national-symbol use is restricted; attorney confirms the line). (ii) **Snakes stay offstage.** The snake-dealing story lives in words only; the mascot never strikes anything on screen (child-safety tone; in some Nguni traditions certain snakes carry ancestral meaning) — and "snake" is never UI vocabulary for weak topics or hard questions (the metaphor would turn an ancestral symbol into "your problems"); in product the bird *walks up to* hard things, the prey stays unnamed. (iii) **Stay far from Duo.** Colour: satisfied by Sentinel's ink body + pink crest (never green-bodied) — natural-colour cues no longer required. Silhouette: still binding — tall, angular, long-legged, never round or chubby (§v2 A5). (iv) **The crest is the emotion dial** (the real bird raises it when alert/displaying): pages fanned open = celebrating ("the book opens"), raised = attentive/waiting, softened = encouraging, lowered = concerned, steady-tall = proud — the fan-open on full marks is the candidate signature interaction (reduced-motion: crossfade). (v) **Favicon rule:** at ≤24px drop to three pages or a single solid crest wedge — thicken, simplify, re-test in one ink. (vi) **Prior art — BirdLife SA's "Strider"** (their 2019 Bird of the Year secretarybird character for learners): never that name; every mascot art pass is compared against it and must read as clearly distinct (TASKS F9 item 6).
2. **Micro-animations — ADOPT (the founder's favorite; our perf budget applies).** Buttons compress and spring back; correct answers celebrate; streak flame flickers; mastery rings fill with overshoot; page transitions slide. CSS transform/opacity + spring easing only (no 3D libs, no Lottie >30KB). Every interactive element has a pressed state. Fluidity is the retention feature.
3. **Illustrations with subtle motion — ADOPT for emotional moments.** Vector characters/scenes (education apps benefit most — the founder's source agrees) for empty states, onboarding, guardian-portal moments, celebrations — always slightly animated so they feel alive. Boundary: §2.6 still rules VALUE moments — heroes that sell the product show REAL past-paper content; illustrations own the FEELING moments.
4. **AI-surface (chatbot) design patterns — ADOPT, adapted to marking.** Our marking flow is an AI surface; apply the genre's best patterns: **streaming mark reveal** (per-step marks appear one by one as if being marked live — kills the ~20s dead wait), skeleton mark-sheet placeholders, "always something happening" progress states, and Perplexity-style **source attribution** (each feedback step cites its memo criterion). Confidence always visibly labeled.
5. **Glassmorphism — RESTRICTED (the founder's own source is skeptical, and it's right).** Real-world readability/accessibility loss beats demo beauty. Allowed ONLY on non-text overlay surfaces (sheets, celebration overlays); NEVER under body text; no Liquid-Glass mimicry on content.

**Standing 2026 rulings (kept from research synthesis):** intentional clarity/schematic data-honest layout — ADOPT (marks look like an honest mark sheet); dark mode first-class — ADOPT (chalkboard identity, battery); adaptive interface — personalize CONTENT, never chrome; accessibility — HARD GATE (AA tokens, 200% text, keyboard nav, focus states; inaccessible PR fails review).
**Rejected:** spatial/AR (device floor) · heavy 3D/textured skeuomorphism (GPU/bundle cost) · mood-theming (inconsistency kills trust) · decorative heroes at value moments (§2.6).

## 2. Decision psychology (founder's A/B brief, mapped to OUR screens)

1. **Ask the easy question.** Never front-load commitment. Onboarding leads with "Try a real exam question" (easy yes), not "Create your account" (homework). Sign-up comes AFTER the first marked answer — the value is felt first.
2. **Transparency timeline = trust.** The guardian-link consent screen uses the trial-timeline pattern: "Today: you link up · Always: they see your effort, your strengths and the school work your teacher marks — your own practice stays yours unless you share it · Anytime: either of you can unlink." Proactively naming the boundary ("your own practice stays yours") is our "we'll remind you before we charge you" — it converts fear into safety.
3. **Verb weight + ownership.** CTAs use light, owning language: "Start my practice", "See my progress", "Link my guardian" — never "Register", "Submit", "Subscribe-weight" words. Numeric specificity beats adjectives: "Marked in ~20 seconds", "2 taps to start", "37 days to Paper 1" — never "fast", "easy", "soon".
4. **Evaluative ease: one number, one choice.** Never show ranges or option walls. Today's practice = ONE recommended set (with a quiet "choose another topic" escape). Results show ONE headline number (4/6) then steps. Three choices maximum anywhere, pre-ranked, recommended one visually primary with a single-word badge ("recommended", "weak spot").
5. **Commitment consistency.** Show the already-made decision as context: the exam target card ("Paper 1 · 4 Nov") sits above every practice prompt — the only question left is "this question now?", not "should I study?".
6. **Transport, don't inform.** Heroes show the real thing: an actual past-paper question snippet, a real marked answer with green ticks, a real mastery ring — never abstract space art. Sensory, specific microcopy: "Real 2024 exam question — marked like the real memo" not "Practice questions available".
7. **Anchor honestly.** We're free — anchor effort, not price: "This paper took the class of 2024 three hours. Your daily set: 15 minutes." Never fake scarcity, never fake discounts — §0 forbids dark patterns; anchoring must be TRUE.

## 3. Engagement mechanics (Duolingo principles, carried from PRD §7)
Progress always visible (streak flame + topic rings in the header) · instant warm feedback on every attempt (celebrate correct; wrong = "here's the exact step" — never shame) · gentle loss-aversion (streak reminder copy is caring, not guilt: "Your 6-day streak misses you") · zero dead ends (every result screen offers ONE next action) · empty states teach ("No practice yet — your first marked answer takes 2 taps").

## 4. Token & component rules (for W3 implementation)
Tokens in `web/src/styles/tokens` (or Tailwind theme): the two brand colors + a derived AA-checked scale (chalkboard 50–950, gold 50–950, semantic success/warn/error), radius 12, spring easing tokens, type scale (display/heading/body/caption, min 16px body). Components (shadcn-based): Button (pressed states, verb-weight naming in stories), Card, ProgressRing, StreakFlame, Celebration overlay, TimelineSteps (consent/OOBE), ChoiceRow (max-3 pattern), MarkSheet (per-step results), **StreamingMarkSheet** (skeleton → per-step streamed reveal with memo citations, §1.4), **Mascot** (5 emotional states per §1.1, silhouette-first until species/name chosen). Every component story shows light+dark and reduced-motion variants.

## 5. Mobbin protocol (for Claude Code during W3+)
Before designing any screen type, pull 3–5 real shipped references from Mobbin for that exact moment (onboarding, results, progress, family-linking, paywall-psychology patterns repurposed for consent) — prefer Duolingo, Photomath, Kahoot, Headspace-class references. Extract the *pattern*, restyle in our tokens. Never copy a screen wholesale; never adopt a pattern that violates §0/§2.

## 6. Design review gate (every UI PR)

## v3 addendum — Ink Marking, Focus Mode, and the evidence rulings (6 Oct 2026)
**Ink Marking (supersedes A1's hollow-marker rendering ON THE MARKED PAGE ONLY; A1 — pink means act, never wrong — remains law for all UI chrome).** The marked result opens with the learner's own page marked in pen (FEATURES §4.10; SCREENS S09/S24): hand-drawn ✓ per mark, ✗ on a lost step, DBE memo codes (A / CA / M / S / R) where the memo uses them, the total circled top-right, margin comments in a legible handwriting face. **Ink tokens (closed set, used only on the marked page):** Green ink `#0B6E3A` (default pen), Red ink `#C8102E` (classic, selectable), Blue ink `#1F3A93` (selectable); applied with ~0.85 opacity and `mix-blend-mode: multiply` over the page so it reads as ink on paper in both themes (the page itself stays light; the dark theme dims the surround, never the paper). **Handwriting face:** candidates Patrick Hand (primary) and Kalam — chosen by the legibility test in TASKS (founder's brothers + ≥10 learners); minimum 18px-equivalent; every ink comment tap-expands to plain text in Outfit; never used for UI text. **Stroke rules:** SVG stroke templates (3–5 variants each for tick, cross, circle, underline), seeded per-attempt jitter so a page re-renders identically, variable width 2–3.5px at 1×, slight overshoot on ticks; placement from the Marker's per-step boxes, margin fallback. Reduced motion: marks appear without the "being drawn" animation; otherwise strokes draw in over ≤300ms each, in step order, matching the streaming reveal.
**Research-driven design laws (RESEARCH_LEARNING_SCIENCE §3–4):** no praise-of-person copy anywhere ("brilliant!" is banned; "this step is learnable" is not) · no normative comparison on a result screen · no leaderboard on a learning surface · struggle-normalising lines may cite real cohort data only · sessions end on a win (peak–end) · confidence check ("sure / not sure") precedes every reveal · "why this matters to the future you chose" sits at the top of weak-topic capsules.
**Focus Mode surface (SCREENS S23):** near-black ground `#111111`, text `#E9E9EC`, a single pink control ("End focus"), timer ring in teal, Sentinel crest-raised in a corner; nothing else. It is the one surface allowed to hide the tab bar.
**Review gate additions (§6):** ☐ no praise-of-person copy ☐ no normative comparison on results ☐ confidence check present before reveal ☐ ink only on the marked page ☐ handwriting comments tap-expand to plain text ☐ guardian surfaces show activity, never work.
