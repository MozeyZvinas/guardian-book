# WHOLE_LEARNER.md — the whole learner: support, people, goals, dreams and opportunities

**v1 · 27 October 2026 · founder-directed, CTO-written, research-backed.**

This document covers everything Guardian Book offers a learner beyond academics: support,
someone to talk to, goals, dreams, hobbies, opportunities, mentors and coaches, and free
and paid help. Each item is ruled on and the reason is given.

Labels:
- **[Proven]** — a fact with a source.
- **[Believed]** — a hypothesis we will test.
- **[Unknown]** — a gap we still have to close.

Every claim cites its source in §11. Where this document and an older one disagree, this
one wins on whole-learner scope; FEATURES.md §4.25–4.31 record the rulings.

---

## 0. Why this exists, and why it was underweighted

The founder's ruling (27 Oct 2026): the intelligence goes beyond academics and beyond
guidance. It covers academic, personal and emotional support, plus a learner's hobbies,
goals and dreams, and it connects learners to people and resources, free and paid.

It was underweighted for four reasons:

1. **On purpose: the founder's earlier steer.** "Not a companion" was taken literally.
   VISION_FULL reserves the word "companion" for the mascot.
2. **On purpose: the hackathon rules.** Meta's list of projects to avoid includes "AI
   mental health advisor", so the wellbeing check-in was cut from the hackathon build.
3. **On purpose: law and evidence.** Both have turned hard against AI companions for
   minors; see §1.
4. **Not on purpose: drift.** Goals, dreams, hobbies, opportunities beyond university
   and bursaries, and human mentors were in the vision (VISION_FULL §3–5). They dropped
   out of every description as the hackathon pitch got tighter, and that undersold
   learners. This document puts them back.

## 1. What the evidence says

### The need is real and under-served

|---|---|---|
| Only **30%** of South African Grade 9s are "never or almost never" bullied, the lowest of the 42 countries with data; the international average is 60%. 22% are bullied about weekly. | TIMSS 2023, exhibit 6.1.10 | [Proven] |
| Learners bullied weekly average **361** in maths, against **422** for those never bullied. | TIMSS 2023, exhibit 6.1.10 | [Proven] |

### General AI fails teenagers on emotional support

| Fact | Source |

### What actually works is human-led, cheap and school-based

| Model | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Shamiri** (Kenya): four group sessions in school, led by trained lay providers, for ages 13–18 | Depression effect size 0.35 straight after, 0.45 at 7 months; anxiety 0.37 and 0.44; about **$15 per student** | JAMA Psychiatry 2021; BMC HSR 2023 |
| **Friendship Bench** (Zimbabwe, adults): six problem-solving sessions with lay health workers | Depression symptoms at 6 months: 14% against 50% | JAMA 2016 |
| **Youth mentoring** (70 studies, 25,286 young people) | Effect size 0.21, or 0.29 after correcting for publication bias: modest and reliable | Raposa et al. 2019 |
| **One stable, caring adult** | The most common factor among resilient children | Harvard Center on the Developing Child |
| **App alone, South Africa** (Kuamsha, rural Mpumalanga) | Feasible, with 80% adherence, but **no effect** | JAACAP Open, Dec 2025 |
| **Growth mindset in the Western Cape** (World Bank) | High-school maths +4.4 to +6.8 points, under $2 per learner; not peer-reviewed | World Bank eMBeD brief |

### Opportunities are large, free and fragmented

| Opportunity | Scale | Source |
|---|---|---|
| SA Mathematics Olympiad | **105,084** entries in 2026 (87,787 in 2025) | SAMF |
| Eskom Expo for Young Scientists | **17,500+** learners in 2025 | Eskom |
| SAYouth.mobi (Harambee) | **4.7 million** young people registered, zero-rated | The Presidency, 2025 |
| YES | **240,668** twelve-month jobs since it launched | yes4youth.co.za |

None of these reaches a learner at the moment their interests, marks and deadlines line up.

### What the evidence decides

1. Learners need support, and the public system can't reach them: one educational
   psychologist per about 55,000 learners.
2. Teenagers already take their feelings to AI, mostly without telling anyone.
3. Open-ended AI emotional support for minors is unsafe today and is becoming illegal
   in our future markets.
4. What works is people: a caring adult, a mentor, a trained lay counsellor, a group in
   school.
5. An app alone does nothing.

So Guardian Book's job is not to be the person. It's to make sure every learner has one,
reaches them fast, and that this person already knows how to help.

## 2. The principle: AI that knows you, people who care for you

> **Guardian Book never pretends to be your friend. It makes sure you're never without
> one.**

How it works:
- **The intelligence:** notices, organises, prepares and connects. It knows the learner's
  goals, gaps, interests and deadlines.
- **People:** hold, counsel, mentor and coach. Those people are:
  - the trusted adults the learner chooses;
  - teachers;
  - mentors, coaches and tutors;
  - trained lay counsellors;
  - registered professionals;
  - crisis lines.
- **The bridge:** the **Brief**. With the learner's agreement, each helper starts already
  knowing what the learner is working towards and what they need.

**The edge:** no tutor site, mentoring programme or helpline can brief a helper from a
verified, living picture of the learner. Guardian Book can, because the Brain is built
from the learner's real work and goals.

## 4. The Care Protocol (binding for every support feature)

1. **Disclosure.** Guardian is always labelled as AI, and it says so whenever a learner
   raises a feeling.
2. **People first.** Every support surface ends in a route to a person, never in more AI.
3. **Short.** No emotional conversation longer than two AI turns. The tutor stays a tutor.
4. **Crisis path.**
   - One tap from anywhere, with tested detection of crisis words in English and
     the main South African languages.
   - A test set of at least 200 cases, including slang and code-switching, run at every
     release.
   - The crisis lines' numbers are confirmed every quarter.
5. **Defaults that protect.** These apply in South Africa too, though the law doesn't
   require them yet:
   - no push notifications at night;
   - sessions that end on purpose;
   - no memory of emotional details;
   - no streaks on support features.

   They mirror SB 1119's defaults.
6. **Data.**
   - Wellbeing data is kept separately, encrypted, minimal and short-lived.
   - It never trains models without explicit consent, and never feeds ads, ranking or
     offers.
   - Learners see and can delete their own data.
7. **Governance.** Before any GUARDED or LATER support feature ships, we need: a
   registered psychologist as clinical advisor, the attorney, a named safeguarding lead,
   an incident log, and Children's Act s110 reporting training for school staff.
8. **Testing.** Every support feature passes a scenario test that the clinical advisor
   signs off, then the founder's phone test.

## 6. Hackathon slice (Meta, 26 Oct – 4 Nov)

What we build (added to META_HACKATHON §4 P1):
1. **Me page and dream → plan** (Muse Spark).
2. **The Opportunity Radar for learners.** A Wonder agent searches the web; every item
   is reviewed by a person before it shows; deadlines flow into the plan. This also
   serves the Best Agentic bonus.
3. **Help now and My people:** static, human-routed, no AI conversation.
4. **The Mentor Brief:** the Brain writes a one-page brief for a fictional mentor; the
   learner approves each line.

What we don't build: check-ins, the counsellor marketplace, peer groups or any emotional
chat.

How we say it: "Guardian Book never pretends to be your friend. It makes sure you're
never without one." It is not an "AI mental health advisor". The AI advises on nothing
emotional; it connects learners to people. All demo data is fictional and labelled.

## 7. The business this opens (details in BUSINESS_PLAN.md)

1. **Care in the whole-school plan,** and an add-on for seat plans (about R40 per learner
   per year, **[Believed]**). It makes the school's existing support team reachable.
2. **Marketplace fees on paid tutors, mentors and coaches:**
   - Fee: about 15–20%. Preply takes 33% down to 18%, Wyzant 25% plus 9%, Outschool 30%,
     and SA agencies more than 50%.
   - Net: about R60 per paid session (estimate).
3. **A flat booking fee for counselling sessions,** not a percentage: HPCSA ethical rule
   7(4) restricts fee-sharing; a legal opinion comes first.
4. **Sponsored support:**
   - Sponsors pay for group programmes, mentoring cohorts or sessions for learners whose
     families can't.
   - Comparables: Talkspace's TeenSpace (New York City, $26m over three years) and Kooth's
     Soluna (California). Governments and districts do pay for youth support platforms
     that keep humans in the loop.
5. **Referral fees on paid resources:** labelled, shown to parents only, never
   pay-to-rank.
